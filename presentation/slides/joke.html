Michael Schumacher's first Formula 1 race

One more thing. Spa-Francorchamps, 1991. A young German gets called in a few days before the race, to drive a Jordan. He qualifies seventh on his debut. And then he doesn't even finish the first lap: the clutch goes. Did not finish. But everybody saw how he drove. Benetton signed him before the next race, a year later he won at Spa, and then he won seven world titles. We didn't finish this one either. See you at the next race. Thank you!
