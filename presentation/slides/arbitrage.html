Our secret idea that actually worked was boring. A dealer sells a card. Another team has already posted a higher bid for that same card. We buy from the dealer and sell into the bid in the same minute. Five trades, plus 116 pesetas, zero losses. Remember why it worked: nobody had to do anything new.
