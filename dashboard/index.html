How the game works, in 60 seconds
  • Only value created scores, never activity or pack luck: 30 negotiating, 30 market-making, 40 judges.
  • Negotiating: dealer deals (how far below the dealer's range we buy, best 3 per level), duels, and value we gain in trades with teams at our values.
  • Market-making: every 2 game hours the Market Test sends every market the same traders; our broker scores the share of gains it realises.
  • Card values are private: book × our set multiplier × copy factor (1st ×1, 2nd ×0.25, 3rd ×0.1), plus a bonus for full pages.
  • Rhythm: one accept and one message per conversation per tick; dealers move only when we move; a final offer is take it or leave it.
  • Each day is a round, averaged; Friday counts half. Ticks: 60 s Fri, 30 s Sat, 15 s Sun.
